**Power Capacitors** are accumulative capital that serve as *potential* mechanisms of [[10 - 19 Power/10. Power\|Power]], which can then be used to either expand or contract boundaries of freedom once converted into [[10 - 19 Power/13. Negotiators\|Negotiators]]. The many different types of Capacitors - economic, social, symbolic, and so on - can be combined in a variety of ways to provide entities with a number of advantages.

![capacitors change to tools](https://dillonbelmont.com/03powermyth-assets/cpct_change.gif)

Capacitors have context-sensitive and relationally determined values. While they can be mixed, there isn't always a clear exchange rate... HOWEVER, classifying each of the types is a good start! So let's dive in.

>[!faq] **Mysteries Yet Uncovered...**
>More information is currently being collected about **Power Capacitors**, and new reports will be posted as they are revealed.

## Economic

**Economic Capacitors** are among the more quantifiable Capacitors. Money, stocks, and other material assets allow folks to fulfill many of life’s basic needs, including food, shelter, and transportation. In a zero-sum economy with finite resources, wealth accumulation is necessarily at the expense of another, so [[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/35. Distributon\|Distributon]] facilitates the allocation of these resources. 

Anyway, here's an interesting website that visualizes disparities: https://realtimeinequality.org/

## Social

**Social capital**. Relationships, friends, networks, club affiliations, etc.

## Informational

**Informational capital** is access to and utilization of knowledge. Is leveraged most frequently for [[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/34. Normaliza\|normalization]] and [[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/36. Repressio\|repression]]. Higher for folks who produce/share knowledge and can set the terms of "truth" for others. Not an exhaustive list but includes:
- Folks who have a wide audience/platform/reach, like the [[50 - 59 Power Conduits/54. Media\|media]] or online [[70 - 79 Cyberspace/73 Enforcers_C/73.07. Influence\|influencers]]
- Those with credibility or formal credentials, like [[50 - 59 Power Conduits/52. Academia\|academia]] or [[50 - 59 Power Conduits/51. Government\|government]]
- Those who exchange their economic capital for it, like advertisers
- Those who make the rules/algorithms on [[70 - 79 Cyberspace/75 Conduits_C/74.01. Platforms\|digital platforms]]

And also, 12GB military documents =/= 12GB of Twilight fanfiction

## Political

**Political capital** is correlated with one's ability to shape [[10 - 19 Power/14. Reactors#Institutions\|institutions]] through law and regulation.
- To super oversimplify it, imagine it as the President having 1,000 political capital and the average voter having like 1.
- This obviously gets way more complicated when you factor in voter initiatives, interest groups, etc... and how economic capital can *often* translate to more political capital but not always

Since it impacts institutions, it can result in significant changes to society's dominant [[10 - 19 Power/11. Realms\|Realm]] directly (e.g., X thing is now illegal) or can impact the accumulation of another Capacitor (e.g., X group now pays fewer taxes).

## Cultural

**Cultural capital** is correlated with one's familiarity with, and ability to shape, [[10 - 19 Power/14. Reactors#Culture\|culture]]. (knowledge, skills, and other cultural acquisitions)

**Symbolic capital** is another write-up that'll take a little while, basically the accumulated prestige/honor.
