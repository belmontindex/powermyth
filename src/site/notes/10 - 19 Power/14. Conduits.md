## Power Conduits

**Every force needs a vessel - and that might even be you.**

**Power Conduits** are avenues of expression for [[10 - 19 Power/10. Power\|Power]]'s mechanisms, embodied as the actors in our social network. Conduits are those that hold [[10 - 19 Power/11. Capacitors\|Capacitors]], are targeted by or employ [[10 - 19 Power/12. Negotiators\|Negotiators]], and reproduce embedded properties as [[10 - 19 Power/13. Emitters\|Reactors]]. Each Conduit has unique attributes and roles, which ascribes particular relationships with different types of Power.
