[[20 - 29 Myths/20. Myths\|Myths]] of **The Villain** broadly paint certain actors in a negative light. The named group may be defined as dangerous, threatening to, or merely different from the originating [[20 - 29 Myths/25. Mythoscapes\|Mythoscape]]. Common examples of the Villain include *the Other*, *the Unworthy*, and *the Savage*.
