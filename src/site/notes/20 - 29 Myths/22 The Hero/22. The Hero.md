## The Hero

[[20 - 29 Myths/20. Myths\|Myths]] of **The Hero** paint actors broadly paint certain actors in a positive light. The named group is usually a stellar representative of the originating [[20 - 29 Myths/25. Mythoscapes\|Mythoscape]], modeling what other members could strive to replicate. Common examples of the Hero include *the Savior*, *the Leader*, and *the Genius*.
