## The Setting

![garden](https://dillonbelmont.com/03powermyth-assets/40/45religion.jpg)
*The Garden of Earthly Delights, Hieronymus Bosch, c. 1490-1510*

[[20 - 29 Myths/20. Myths\|Myths]] of **The Setting** explain how the world and its structures came to be, typically leaning on a combination of history and the perspective of the originating [[20 - 29 Myths/25. Mythoscapes\|Mythoscape]]. These descriptions can invoke past, present, or future to either justify the status quo or prove the need for change. Common examples of the Setting include *the Paradise*, *the Dystopia*, and *the Struggle*.
