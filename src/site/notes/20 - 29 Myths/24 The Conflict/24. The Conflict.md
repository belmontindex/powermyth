## The Conflict

![name](https://dillonbelmont.com/03powermyth-assets/40/40conduits.jpg)
*The Triumph of Death, Pieter Bruegel the Elder, c. 1562*

[[20 - 29 Myths/20. Myths\|Myths]] of **The Conflict** describe the stakes of an existing situation, setting the political and moral agenda of the originating [[20 - 29 Myths/25. Mythoscapes\|Mythoscape]]. Common examples of the Conflict include *the Doom*, *the Retribution*, and *the Persecution*.
