## Mythdirection

If [[20 - 29 Myths/20. Myths\|Myths]] are shortcuts for our brains, what happens when someone changes the off ramp? **Mythdirection** is the process by which an actor leverages expectations associated with Myths and [[20 - 29 Myths/25. Mythoscapes\|Mythoscapes]] to accomplish a different goal than was originally intended. The gap between style and substance can be exploited to provide the appearance of legitimacy and a chance to redirect the underlying functions of norms.
