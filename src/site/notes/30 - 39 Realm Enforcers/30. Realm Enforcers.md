## Realm Enforcers

Each time individuals enter into a collective, a brand new [[10 - 19 Power/11. Realms\|Realm]] is forged. Yet unlike those tied to the corporeal force of individuals, these collective Realms are inherently unstable due to the contradictory elements found among the many Realms they're composed of. *Something* must stabilize them...

The six **Realm Enforcers** represent these social forces that ensure the [[10 - 19 Power/14. Reactors\|Power Reactors]] for a given collective stay on their current path:

**[[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/31. Monadus\|Mondaus]]**, the leader.

**[[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/32. Mithrot\|Mithrot]]**, the storyteller.

**[[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/33. Categorius\|Categorius]]**, the evaluator.

**[[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/34. Normaliza\|Normaliza]]**, the peacemaker.

**[[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/35. Distributon\|Distributon]]**, the decider.

**[[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/36. Repressio\|Repressio]]**, the enforcer.
