## Monadus

**Monadus** appears to be leader among the [[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/30. Realm Enforcers\|Realm Enforcers]], as this deity is associated with the processes that maintain a set order in a collective. Those who have written of the concept of *hegemony* seem to have identified the complex effects of Monadus:

>[!quote] **Stuart Hall,** *Cultural Studies 1983: A Theoretical History* (2013, p. 171)
>It can not overcome all of the fundamental contradictions within the structure of the society in which it is established... But it is capable of taking the leading, defining position. It is capable of a certain mastery of the terrain.
