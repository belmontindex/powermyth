## Monadus

**Monadus** appears to be leader among the [[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/30. Realm Enforcers\|Realm Enforcers]], as this deity is responsible for defining the frame of reference used to stabilize a collective.

Society is composed of many layers of overlapping, yet often contradictory, [[10 - 19 Power/14. Reactors\|Reactors]]. In order for this or any collective to function, the terrain must share a common language to narrow down what is or isn't acceptable. In limiting the range of possibilities, Monadus also limits the extent of potential change to society's dominant [[10 - 19 Power/11. Realms\|Realm]].
