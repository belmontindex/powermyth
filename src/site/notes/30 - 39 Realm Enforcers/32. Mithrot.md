## Mithrot

If [[20 - 29 Myths/20. Myths\|Myths]] are shortcuts for our brains, what happens when someone changes the off ramp? 

**Mithrot**'s role among the [[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/30. Realm Enforcers\|Realm Enforcers]] is to leverage expectations associated with Myths and [[20 - 29 Myths/25. Mythoscapes\|Mythoscapes]], often in such a way that they accomplish goals different than what was originally intended. Those who have written of concepts such as appropriation and co-optation seem to have identified residual effects of Mithrot.
