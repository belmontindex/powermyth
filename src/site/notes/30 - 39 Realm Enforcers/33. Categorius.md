## Categorius

**Categorius** is the [[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/30. Realm Enforcers\|Realm Enforcer]] responsible for the classification and evaluation of that which is "good" or "bad" in society. Subjectification and the construction of social categories seem to be residual effects of Categorius.
