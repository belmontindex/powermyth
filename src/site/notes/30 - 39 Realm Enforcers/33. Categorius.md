## Categorius

**Categorius** is the [[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/30. Realm Enforcers\|Realm Enforcer]] responsible for the classification of knowledge. *What is a thing?*
Critically, this makes large volumes of information more manageable to parse.

The monotony of Categorius' role masks his impact, however. The relation of objects to others changes the properties of each. Categories are not neutral, either: moral evaluations, that which is "good" or "bad," are embedded within. What could this mean when categories for ideas - or people - are taken for granted?
