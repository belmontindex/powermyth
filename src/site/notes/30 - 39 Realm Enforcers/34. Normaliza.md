## Normaliza

**Normaliza** makes the unusual and irrational appear unquestionable. Her role among the [[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/30. Realm Enforcers\|Realm Enforcers]] is to remind the collective "this is just the way it is." 

But should it be? What does this mean for possibilities of changing social structures when individuals must steadily participate in systems that so heavily disadvantage them?
