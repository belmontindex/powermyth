## Normaliza

**Normaliza** makes the unusual and irrational appear normal. This is a critical role among the [[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/30. Realm Enforcers\|Realm Enforcers]] if individuals must steadily participate in systems that so heavily disadvantage them. Those who have written of internalization and symbolic violence seem to have identified residual effects of Normaliza.
