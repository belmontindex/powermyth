## Repressio

**Repressio**'s role among the [[30 - 39 Realm Enforcers/30. Realm Enforcers\|Realm Enforcers]] is to prevent attempts to change society's dominant [[10 - 19 Power/11. Realms\|Realm]]. While it occasionally silences opponents through brute force, Repressio considers itself more successful when its targets don't even realize they've been targeted in the first place.
