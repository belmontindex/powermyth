## Realm Enforcers

Each time individuals enter into a collective, a brand new [[30 - 39 Realms/30. Realms\|Realm]] is forged. Yet unlike those tied to the corporeal force of individuals, these collective Realms are inherently unstable due to the contradictory elements found among the many Realms they're composed of. *Something* must stabilize them...

The six **Realm Enforcers** represent these social forces that ensure the [[10 - 19 Power/14. Reactors\|Power Reactors]] for a given collective stay on their current path:

**[[40 - 49 Realm Enforcers/41. Monadus\|Mondaus]]**, the leader.

**[[40 - 49 Realm Enforcers/42. Mithrot\|Mithrot]]**, the storyteller.

**[[40 - 49 Realm Enforcers/43. Categorius\|Categorius]]**, the evaluator.

**[[40 - 49 Realm Enforcers/44. Normaliza\|Normaliza]]**, the peacemaker.

**[[40 - 49 Realm Enforcers/45. Distributon\|Distributon]]**, the decider.

**[[40 - 49 Realm Enforcers/46. Repressio\|Repressio]]**, the enforcer.
