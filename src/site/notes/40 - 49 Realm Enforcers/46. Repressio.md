## Repressio

**Repressio**'s role among the [[40 - 49 Realm Enforcers/40. Realm Enforcers\|Realm Enforcers]] is to prevent attempts to change a collective's dominant [[30 - 39 Realms/30. Realms\|Realm]]. While it occasionally silences opponents through brute force, Repressio considers itself more successful when its targets don't even realize they've been targeted in the first place.
