## Power Conduits
Though actors - whether individuals, groups, or entities - do not *hold* [[10 - 19 Power/10. Power\|Power]], they do still serve as vessels for and wielders of it. These **Power Conduits** are inherently neutral, though they are avenues for both domination and liberation.

For our case, the unique role each Conduit has in society ascribes particular strengths and weaknesses for different types of Power to manifest, so understanding these attributes is worthy of study.
