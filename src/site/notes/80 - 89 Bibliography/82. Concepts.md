**[[Pleromism\|Pleromism]]** was developed largely through reconciling contributions from a number of different theoretical concepts. This page tries its best to make reference to as many of them as I possibly can... but that comes with a big disclaimer!

**Inclusion on this page does not indicate complete alignment**. In fact, most of the concepts listed here have been further explored, redefined, and expanded on by a bunch of people since their inception anyway. Some interpretations won't click. So just think of this as a directory of other ideas that cover similar ground in case you want to dive deeper into any particular area!

This page is one angle describing influences to Pleromism. Be sure to also check out the companion [[80 - 89 Bibliography/83. People\|People]] page, the broader [[80 - 89 Bibliography/81 Discourses/81. Discourses\|Discourses]] that contextualize the lineage of these ideas, and the full [[80 - 89 Bibliography/80 Resources/80.X Lineage Outline\|Lineage Outline]].
