**[[Pleromism\|Pleromism]]** was developed largely through reconciling contributions from a number of different individuals and fields of study. This page tries its best to make reference to as many of them as I possibly can... but that comes with a big disclaimer!

**Inclusion on this page does not indicate complete alignment**. Most of the people/fields listed here have such a huge body of work that there's no way I could possibly agree with everything they've said, let alone pretend to be aware of it all. Instead, think of this as a directory of some other sources who talk about similar ideas in case you want to dive deeper into any particular area!

This page is one angle describing influences to Pleromism. Be sure to also check out the companion [[80 - 89 Bibliography/82. Concepts\|Concepts]] page, the broader [[80 - 89 Bibliography/81 Discourses/81. Discourses\|Discourses]] that contextualize the lineage of these ideas, and the full [[80 - 89 Bibliography/80 Resources/80. Bibliography\|Bibliography]].
