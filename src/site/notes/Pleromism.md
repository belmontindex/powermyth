## Pleromism

This research emerged from a desperate attempt to find out whether significant social change is really possible. 

We are simultaneously faced with an impending [climate disaster](https://www.jstor.org/stable/26891410), an extreme [concentration of wealth](https://wir2022.wid.world/executive-summary/) contrasted by a growing population [sleeping outside](https://www.huduser.gov/portal/sites/default/files/pdf/2024-AHAR-Part-1.pdf), and technology that [evolves at a faster rate](https://pdf.stafforini.com/collingridge-1980-social-control-technology.pdf) than we can reasonably control. Try as one might to redirect this trajectory (and good luck to them), individual efforts can hardly make a dent in issues so deeply embedded in the fabric of society. Are we prisoners to the wants of our ancestors and kings, destined toward some catastrophic fate?

Even the hope for collective effort consistently seems bleak. When competing factions across ideological lines overwhelmingly assume the other [disagrees with basic facts](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/30/most-americans-say-republican-and-democratic-voters-cannot-agree-on-basic-facts/), is it even possible to work together? Flurries of public debate are led by institutions that few trust, whether it be [the media](https://news.gallup.com/poll/695762/trust-media-new-low.aspx) or [legislative chambers](https://news.gallup.com/poll/1600/congress-public.aspx).  It has been [over twenty years](https://news.gallup.com/poll/1669/general-mood-country.aspx) since the last time more than half of Americans have felt positively about the direction of the country; these days, it's less than a quarter. Transparently, I'm somewhat skeptical change is truly on the table, but giving up isn't an option, either!

I believe our path forward can be revealed if we have a systematic approach to understanding two processes. First, **what is the social order, and what shapes it?** Then, **what enables or prevents change?** Both allow us to evaluate existing circumstances and consider what capacity must be built to foster a more equitable society. This is the blueprint of **Pleromism**.

# 1. Order

In social life, there are a given set of boundaries that define what is good, possible, rational, normal, and so on. These boundaries constitute a **[[30 - 39 Realms/30. Realms\|REALM]]**.

![realms define good, legal, normal](https://dillonbelmont.com/03powermyth-assets/Realm.gif)

Boundaries may be explicit or implicit. Regardless, we know these boundaries to exist because when individuals act outside of them, they do so at their expense: If someone breaks the law, they are punished; if someone acts weird or unethically, there are social consequences. This is the nature of the social contract.

But while consequences might be more visible in a collective, they can also be self-imposed. In other words, REALMS exist at scale. Each individual has their own sense of good and rational that may not completely align with that of the collective. Forming the collective REALM of society, then, means some of the contradicting interests that naturally occur in a group are either reconciled or excluded.

![collective realms exclude necessarily](https://dillonbelmont.com/03powermyth-assets/collective2.gif)

These boundaries are perpetually scrutinized to more closely reflect the general will of the collective. Attempts to negotiate them target various **[[10 - 19 Power/14. Conduits\|CONDUITS]]** in society, which convey and uphold the boundaries at any given point. CONDUITS are commonly cultural expressions or institutional entities: Congress, language, art, family, religion, media, science, and any other construct that plays a role distinguishing between what is inside or outside the REALM.

CONDUITS (much like the individual REALMS that form the collective) are not perfectly in agreement; that is to be expected. They cannot be understood in isolation, though. They influence one another and co-produce the processes that inform daily life, and the interrelated network is the social order.

![conduits absorb mechanics of power](https://dillonbelmont.com/03powermyth-assets/conduits2.gif)

The dominant configuration of CONDUITS at any given time--the positions of these elements that make up the complex whole of society--is a *Pleroma*.

In an equitable society, such a configuration would most closely represent the interests of all individuals who are a part. Were boundaries democratically drawn by all members of a collective REALM, and did the previously described reconciliation and exclusion occur under mutual terms? Or were certain subsets of the population denied the right to vote? Were beliefs reproduced without intention when implicitly acquired at a young age? 

Without fair opportunity for all to knowingly shape the boundaries that constrain them, domination occurs. This is the state of *False Pleroma*. 

# 2. Change

If the dominant configuration of CONDUITS did not simply occur out of mutual agreement between parties involved in a collective, what determined their position? To answer this, we must turn to **[[10 - 19 Power/10. Power\|POWER]]** and its many manifestations.

![power defines what's possible](https://dillonbelmont.com/03powermyth-assets/power_full.gif)

Certain properties offer the *potential* to reshape the boundaries of possibility. Wealth, status, and information are a few subtypes of POWER **[[10 - 19 Power/11. Capacitors\|CAPACITORS]]** that can be accumulated to determine the influence and autonomy of actors. As is the case with capital, CAPACITORS can accumulate negatively as well as positively.

![capacitors change to tools](https://dillonbelmont.com/03powermyth-assets/type1.gif)

Then, that potential can be converted into active expressions of POWER. **[[10 - 19 Power/12. Negotiators\|NEGOTIATORS]]** represent the mechanisms that expand or contract the boundaries of possibility, e.g., passing a new law, paying off a debt, or creating an artistic expression.

The scale of NEGOTIATORS occurs at either an individual or collective level, the latter of which is usually a result of targeting CONDUITS. Given the diversity of individual REALMS in a collective, it is often the case that these mechanisms do not have universal application (what expands in one direction may require contraction in another).

![power shifts the boundaries](https://dillonbelmont.com/03powermyth-assets/negotiators2.gif)

Lastly, in the event that mechanisms of POWER are embedded in CONDUITS, they are perpetually reflected back to the collective as **[[10 - 19 Power/13. Emitters\|EMITTERS]]**. Passive effects from cultures and institutions, such as laws, norms, and customs, reproduce the actions that shaped them.

![cultures and institutions maintain boundaries](https://dillonbelmont.com/03powermyth-assets/emitters.gif)

If a discriminatory law continually restricts access to CAPACITORS to those who would otherwise change it, or if social expectations ostracize those who think differently... existing configurations can be reinforced indefinitely. 

# 3. Limits

So, we are subjects of a world where what we can or cannot do is defined by the meanings embedded in a network of the cultures and institutions that we are born into, which are in some ways outside of our immediate control. Yet simultaneously, we were the agents responsible for shaping this network in the first place! Why do we not then pursue a more equitable REALM?

Variations are embodied through fields of knowledge and behaviors called **[[20 - 29 Myths/25. Mythoscapes\|Mythoscapes]]**. Mythoscapes are relationally defined and operate at the following levels:
- **[[20 - 29 Myths/26 Self Mythoscapes/26. Self\|Self]]**: characteristics and identity, such as race, class, gender, or age.
- **[[20 - 29 Myths/27 Club Mythoscapes/27. Club\|Club]]**: affiliations and community, such as region, occupation, or political party.
- **[[20 - 29 Myths/28 World Mythoscapes/28. World\|World]]**: purported universals, such as being, truth, or reality.

Mythoscapes generally come with their own set of **[[20 - 29 Myths/20. Myths\|Myths]]** that help rationalize the world around us, each with unique stories of who should be disliked ([[20 - 29 Myths/21 The Villain/21. The Villain\|The Villain]]) and who should be rooted for ([[20 - 29 Myths/22 The Hero/22. The Hero\|The Hero]]). They also set the stage ([[20 - 29 Myths/23 The Setting/23. The Setting\|The Setting]]) and describe what the heroes are up against ([[20 - 29 Myths/24 The Conflict/24. The Conflict\|The Conflict]]).

![myths explain our lives](https://dillonbelmont.com/03powermyth-assets/20/mythscroll16.gif)

The Mythoscapes that a person is either ascribed or subscribes to, perhaps unsurprisingly, are also unlikely to perfectly overlap. The process by which Mythoscapes are reconciled to form an individual Realm mirrors the process by which individual Realms are reconciled to form a [[30 - 39 Realms/32. Collective Realms\|collective Realm]]: Both levels see necessary exclusions, clear priorities, and some amount of unresolved squishiness.

In any given moment, there is a limit to the scope of social change in a collective borne out of the function of ensuring its stability. Such processes are typically invisible or otherwise taken for granted.

When a collective forges a new Realm, the contradictions within result in some inherent instability. Excluded components have incentive to expand the boundaries, but constant negotiations threaten the Realm's composition. Therefore, taken-for-granted social forces known as **[[40 - 49 Realm Enforcers/40. Realm Enforcers\|Realm Enforcers]]** prevent significant change from occurring:
- **[[40 - 49 Realm Enforcers/41. Monadus\|Monadus]], the leader** who defines the dominant position.
- **[[40 - 49 Realm Enforcers/42. Mithrot\|Mithrot]], the storyteller** who imbues meaning in myths.
- **[[40 - 49 Realm Enforcers/43. Categorius\|Categorius]], the evaluator** who classifies information.
- **[[40 - 49 Realm Enforcers/44. Rationaliza\|Rationaliza]], the peacemaker** who justifies the way it is.
- **[[40 - 49 Realm Enforcers/45. Distributon\|Distributon]], the decider** who allocates resources.
- **[[40 - 49 Realm Enforcers/46. Repressio\|Repressio]], the silencer** who prevents what is unacceptable.
